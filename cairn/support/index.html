How does Cairn work?

Each morning you set one word for the day. Each evening you can add one sentence reflecting on it. That's the whole ritual — small on purpose.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. Cairn syncs through your own private iCloud account, so your words appear on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Make sure you're signed into iCloud and that iCloud is enabled for Cairn in your device settings.

My Apple Watch isn't showing today's word.

Sync between devices can take a moment, especially right after installing. Make sure both devices are on the same iCloud account and connected to the internet. Opening the app on each device helps it sync sooner.

Is my data private?

Completely. Cairn collects nothing — no analytics, no tracking. Your entries live only on your device and in your private iCloud, which we can't access. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I add a widget?

Long-press your Home Screen or Lock Screen, tap the + (or Customize), search for "Cairn," and add the widget you'd like.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting individual entries is done inside the app. To remove everything, delete the app and remove its data from iCloud in your device's iCloud settings.
